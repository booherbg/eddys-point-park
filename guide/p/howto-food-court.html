Eddy's Park Guide / How to

Place a food court

Paint a food court beside a path and vendors move in. Where you put it decides which ones.

A park can have more than one court.

Plants, water, seats, lamps and a clean court earn it stars. The park's rating adds stars of its own.

The money a court makes is the park's.

A vendor that sells too little leaves, and another kind moves in. A quiet corner keeps changing vendors.

Each vendor comes more often where it sells best.
